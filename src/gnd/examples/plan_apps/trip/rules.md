# Trip planning rules

- Hotels and activities together stay within the trip's budget.
- A trip has one hotel a night.
- Every night booked falls within the trip, before its last day.
- A trip has one activity at a time.

## What the planner does

- Book a hotel for a night.
- Book an activity.
