# Generic family words

The LI3 spec's family classifier used any shared token of length four or
more. Corpus inspection shows that literal carrier nouns can recur across
unrelated names such as AlphaFold family and Boltz family. The noun
describes how the name is used and supplies no name identity on its own.

Exclude the normalized tokens family, method and variant from the shared
family-token signal. Keep the other family predicates and original values.
A case sharing only the excluded tokens must have no family candidate.
The PINN case continues to qualify through its PINN token.

This clarification is recorded before running the measurement. The
committed corpus, labels, selection rule, threshold, and budget retain
their original digests and approval.
