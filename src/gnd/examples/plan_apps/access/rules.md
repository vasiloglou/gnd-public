# Access rules

- Each employee reports to one manager.
- Nobody manages themselves, even through a chain of managers.
- Nobody approves access for someone they manage, directly or through a chain
  of managers.
- Nobody approves their own access.
- Access is granted only after it is approved.

## What the access desk does

- Approve an employee's access to a system (the approver is recorded).
- Grant access, once approved.
- Revoke access.
