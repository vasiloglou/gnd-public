# Pharmacy refill rules

- Only prescribed drugs are refilled.
- A refill can't be given before the drug's refill interval has passed since
  the last refill.
- A patient never gets two drugs that interact on the same day, whichever way
  round the interaction is listed.
- A patient should not go more than 60 days between refills (a guideline: the
  checker warns, it doesn't refuse).
- The pharmacist uses judgement for anything the rules don't cover (not
  something a checker can check; it is listed as not checked).

## What the pharmacy does

- Refill a drug for a patient on a day.
